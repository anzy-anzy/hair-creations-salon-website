Meet Araceli

Color is the craft. People are the point.

Hair Creations Salon & Spa sits in Fiesta Plaza on San Felipe Road, where Araceli and her team have built a reputation around one simple idea: a salon visit should feel unhurried, personal, and a little bit like catching up with a friend who happens to be excellent at hair.

Araceli's specialty is color — rich balayage, seamless highlights, careful gray blending, and color correction for hair that's been through a rough patch elsewhere. She listens before she creates, which is a big part of why clients come back year after year, and why so many of Hollister's bridal parties and quinceañeras trust her with the biggest hair-and-makeup day of their lives.

The salon has grown into a spa, too, with a private treatment room for facials and waxing — so a visit here can be about unwinding just as much as it's about a new look.
