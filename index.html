Meet Your Stylist

Discover the Hair Creations difference

Clients come to Hair Creations for color that's done right — rich balayage, seamless highlights, and gray-free blends — paired with the kind of unhurried, personal service a boutique salon is built on. Araceli listens first, then creates: from everyday color refreshes to full bridal transformations, succulent-crown up-dos, and the makeup artistry (maquillaje y peinado) the salon is known for around Hollister.

The salon doubles as a spa, with a private treatment room for facials and waxing — so a visit here can be as much about unwinding as it is about a new look.
